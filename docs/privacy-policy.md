# Capture privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Capture (listed on the App Store as "Todo Agent") is a personal task-capture app for iOS, macOS and
the web, published by Chris Mitchelmore. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and
who it's shared with.

## Data we collect

| Data | Why | Linked to you |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and password hash | Account sign-in and sync between your devices | Yes |
| Tasks, notes, tags, categories, rules and memory notes you create | Storing and syncing your tasks; AI suggestions and research | Yes |
| Images you attach to tasks | Storing and syncing your tasks | Yes |
| Crash reports and performance traces (Sentry) | Fixing bugs and keeping the app reliable | No |

We don't use your data for advertising or tracking, and we don't sell it.

## Data that stays on your device

- **Calendar:** with your permission, Capture reads your calendar's busy times on your device to
  check whether a due date is realistic. Calendar events are never uploaded.
- **Preferences** such as appearance and Obsidian settings are stored only on your device.

## AI processing (OpenAI)

Capture uses OpenAI to suggest categories, summarise links and research tasks. The app asks for
your permission before using it. When you capture or update a task, the Capture server sends OpenAI:

- the task title and any research instructions;
- links in the task and the text of those pages;
- memory notes you've saved for research.

OpenAI doesn't receive your email address, password or calendar. OpenAI processes this data under
its API terms, which don't allow it to train its models on API data by default. You can withdraw
permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & AI; Capture then stops sending new tasks from that
device.

## Service providers

- **Hosting** for the sync server and database (currently a server operated by the publisher).
- **PowerSync** software for syncing data between your devices, run on the same server.
- **OpenAI** for the AI processing described above.
- **Sentry** for crash and performance reports.
- **Email delivery** provider for sign-in codes and password resets.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all of its data, open
Settings → Account → Delete Account in the iPhone, iPad, Mac or web app. Deletion is immediate and
removes your tasks, history, attachments, memories, sessions and sign-in credentials from the
server. If you can't sign in, email the address below from your account's email address and we'll
delete it within 30 days. Deleting a task in the app removes it from sync.

## Children

Capture isn't directed at children under 13.
